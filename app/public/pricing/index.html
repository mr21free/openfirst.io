The promise behind the prices. If a feature protects your family, it's free — encryption, locks, the reader, the format. Paid tiers buy breadth (more plans), history (versioning), and comfort (templates, scores, support). If we ever disappear, everything you paid for keeps working: it runs on your device and your license is a file, not a login.

Questions you should ask

Why one-time and not a subscription?
Because this product must outlive us, and you shouldn't have to bet on that. A subscription to a 30-year promise is the wrong deal — roughly forty of our competitors proved it. You pay once; the software is local; done.
Do I need an account?
No. There are no accounts. Payment happens at checkout with the payment provider; you receive a signed license file that the app verifies offline. We couldn't track you if we wanted to.
What happens to my plan if I stop paying?
Nothing — there's nothing to stop paying. And the Free tier is never a trial: one full plan, full security, forever.
Is the heir's file ever paid?
Never. The reader your family opens is free, open source, and works offline, whoever built the plan.
