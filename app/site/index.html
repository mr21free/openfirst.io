Why this exists

She shouldn't have to solve your life like a puzzle.

When something happens, your partner becomes detective, accountant, and IT admin overnight — while grieving. OpenFirst turns what's in your head into a runbook: what exists, where it is, who helps, what to do first.

A plan you never tested is a hope, not a plan.

You told her the password. She still couldn't open it — true story, ours. OpenFirst has dry runs: sit together, let her actually find, open, and follow the plan, and record what broke.

Made to outlive us. Even the company.

One open file: plain text and files inside, strong encryption if you want it, provably offline — the reader physically cannot phone home. If OpenFirst disappears tomorrow, your plan still opens in 2046.

How it works
  1. Build the map — people, the places things live, items (accounts, wallets, keys, documents), files, and calm plain-language guides. Your own AI can scaffold it in minutes.
  2. Test it before it matters — run a dry run with the person who'd use it. Watch where they hesitate. Fix that.
  3. Hand over one file — export a self-contained start-here.html (optionally encrypted), put it where they'd look, and tell them it exists.
